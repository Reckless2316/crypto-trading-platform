## One atomic economic operation

For each verified fill/flow/correction, one synchronous authority-owned transaction must bind immutable source identity/content, journal postings, native movements, checked weighted-average basis projections at the new journal watermark, economic reservation changes, account revision/readiness, and applicable marks/control transitions. Include preflow mark, unit change and HWM/day/latch state in the flow transaction. Failure at any write rolls everything back; persistence uncertainty disables dispatch. No network or await inside the transaction.

New economics invalidate earlier reconciliation/valuation readiness. Reenable it only against complete matching evidence, never an old `reconcile_id` plus new balances. Task 2 reports a transaction/invalidation seam; consult the final Task 2 handoff before wiring it. Do not bypass immutable records with direct mutable balance patches.

## Fill, basis and fee economics

Use the verified venue definitions of actual base delivery, quote settlement and fee inclusion. Do not assume `base_quantity * price` equals the separately reported settled quote quantity exactly; venue rounding must have bounded documented treatment. Unknown or inconsistent semantics inhibit readiness. Every asset movement has its corresponding external settlement/fee leg: native conservation is per asset, not a sum of unlike asset quantities. Reconcile every owned holding, including assets outside strategy candidates and fee assets.

With USD quote, positive gross notional `N`, trade basis `B`, USD fee `F`, the architecture requires these signed postings:

| Operation | USD journal |
|---|---|
| Buy | inventory `+N`; fee expense `+F`; cash `-(N+F)` |
| Sell | cash `+(N-F)`; fee expense `+F`; inventory `-B`; realized profit `-(N-B)` |

Post actual native quantities alongside these USD legs. USD fees are expensed once rather than capitalized and expensed again. Sell basis is the weighted-average basis attributable to actual sold units; a complete disposal removes the remaining basis exactly. Partial disposals conserve total basis using explicit rounding policy and residual allocation. Reject unknown opening basis, insufficient owned quantity and negative holdings; do not clamp them to zero.

For a non-USD fee of `f` units, obtain a verified USD fee value `V` and remove the fee asset's weighted-average basis `C`. A separate fee-disposal leg is inventory `-C`, fee expense `+V`, realized profit `-(V-C)`, with native asset delta `-f`. This balances independently. It applies to third assets and gives a consistent treatment for base-paid fees; buy/sell native accounting must not subtract an already net-reported fee twice. If a buy acquires base and pays its fee from that delivery, explicitly determine and record acquisition/disposal sequencing before allocating basis. For a sell with an extra base fee, availability must cover sold base plus fee. Do not charge a USD cash fee as well unless the evidence contains a separate charge.

Fee/conversion evidence must identify the asset/pair, units/rate direction, USD value, source and content identity, causal event/receipt/knowledge times, valuation time, freshness and applicable precision. Preserve conversion legs when no direct USD pair is used. USDC/USDT remain assets requiring verified conversion; parity is not evidence. Missing conversion/basis/final fees makes accounting unready and keeps outstanding fee capacity reserved. Record confirmed principal economics without inventing a final zero fee.

Each transaction balances integer USD microdollars to zero, has checked int64 postings/projections, and explicitly accounts for only bounded conversion/basis rounding residuals. Never route unexplained venue balance differences into rounding, P&L or external capital.

## Partial fills, late fills and corrections

- Bind dedupe to account/venue/trade identity **and** immutable content. Identical retries have no additional economic effect; changed base/quote/fee content is a correction incident. Local fixture hashes must actually bind retained canonical payloads.
- Consume only confirmed cash/base/notional from the original reservation and retain worst-case remaining quantity, final-fee bounds and pending-loss budget. Recompute remaining exposure/loss conservatively; do not simply drop all budgets on the first partial fill. Owned equity still includes reserved cash. Status alone releases nothing.
- A fill after CANCELED/FILLED still journals once. Cumulative disagreement creates an incident and inhibits entries. UNKNOWN/cancel acknowledgment/single not-found never establishes releasable remainder. Task 13 supplies terminal status plus reconciled cumulative quantities and final fees.
- Preserve original fill/mark evidence. A verified correction binds its predecessor content and unique correction identity, appends compensating journal postings, and rebuilds current balances/basis/reservations and affected valuation findings. Duplicate correction is a no-op only for identical content. Reject stale/conflicting correction chains.
- Correcting an older acquisition can change basis allocated to later sales and therefore later realized P&L; replay the dependent projection history and compensate the current difference rather than reversing only one isolated cash leg. Keep historical marks immutable and append findings/current marks. A favorable correction cannot clear an earlier drawdown latch.

## Liquidation equity, flows and daily state

`E = owned USD cash + executable liquidation value of every held asset - liabilities - remaining exit costs`. Require sufficient current executable depth for the entire holding, verified conversions and explicit fees/slippage convention. Do not mark with mid/last price alone or silently value unknown holdings at zero. Already charged fees reduce cash/native holdings and are not deducted a second time from equity. Reservations reduce spendable capacity, not owned equity.

At verified initialization require positive reconciled `E,U` and valid baseline evidence. For a verified external capital flow, compute `n=E/U`, `delta_U=F/n`, then `E'=E+F`, `U'=U+delta_U`; preserve preflow NAV, HWM and existing incidents. Separate transfer costs/valuation losses from capital. Retain transfer identity, native amount, verified USD value and preflow mark; unexplained balance differences are not deposits. Persist factual flow evidence even if arithmetic/valuation cannot produce a ready projection.

Full withdrawal is a separate closure operation before division by zero. Record the withdrawal and durable closed/unready state while retaining units/history and latches needed for audit; do not feed zero NAV into the positive-NAV helper or silently reseed a fresh account. Redeposit/reopen/release/restart cannot erase drawdown or review incidents.

Maintain `H=max(previous H,n)` from admissible marks, never lower it after a flow or favorable correction. Daily opening NAV belongs to the UTC boundary and must have valid causal valuation evidence. A later favorable mark cannot be relabeled midnight. If the process missed the boundary, restore it only from genuine boundary evidence; otherwise remain unready. Rollover can end the previous day's daily inhibition only with the new valid opening mark and reconciliation. Drawdown/crisis/review/hard-kill latches and restart's unarmed state survive. Missing initialization/boundary evidence cannot be manufactured from a convenient positive value.

Readiness requires complete verified holdings/basis/fees/flows, positive equity/units/baselines, current depth/conversions, completed matching reconciliation and genuine private heartbeat evidence. Current architecture defaults: public book 2s, private heartbeat 5s, reconciliation 15s; Task 13 provides the acquisition. Reject future timestamps/causal inconsistencies and gaps. Fresh process uptime is not a venue heartbeat. Economic readiness never arms the account or authorizes reductions.

## Exact arithmetic and loss boundaries

`loss_fractions` returns reporting fractions `max(0,1-n/H)` and `max(0,1-n/D)` after validating finite positive Decimal inputs. Use a local deterministic context; never inherit the caller's default precision. Trigger drawdown iff `100*n <= 85*H`, daily iff `100*n <= 98*D`, using unrounded authoritative values. Equality triggers. Persist triggers atomically with the mark/economic event, even while unarmed, without a model call. Entry cancellation/authorized reducing transport remains with authority; setting a halt does not itself place an exit.

Decimal multiplication also rounds under a context. For bounded finite Decimal coefficients, use exact integer coefficient/exponent comparisons or sufficient proven precision rather than assuming `prec=50` makes every cross-product exact. If NAV is represented by the exact ratio `E/U`, compare `100*E <= 85*H*U` and `100*E <= 98*D*U`; if historical H/D are ratios too, cross-multiply their denominators as well. Do not substitute a rounded stored/report NAV for that authority.

The brief's 50-digit arithmetic example and frozen 38-digit/scale Decimal serialization need explicit integration treatment: repeating NAV/unit issuance cannot be persisted unchanged. Use exact persisted rational authority and compatible bounded Decimal projections. Ordinary repeating deposits must work; treating every repeating division as unsupported would fail the intended unitization behavior. A money-rounding posting cannot conceal issuance/redemption drift. Silent unit truncation, widening contracts implicitly, floats, or rounding fractions before authorization are unacceptable. No controlling specification/architecture contradiction was found; this is a representational design obligation, not permission to weaken the requirements.

### Proposed minimal extensions for conductor authorization before implementation

1. Add an append-only exact-mark companion keyed by the concrete `equity_marks.mark_id` FK. Store reduced signed integer numerator/positive denominator pairs for units, NAV, high-water NAV and day-open NAV, with account identity, evidence hash and recorded time. Existing `equity_usd_u` remains the authoritative conservatively rounded USD equity; assert exact NAV equals `(equity_usd_u / 1_000_000) / exact_units`. Store an exact-flow companion keyed by `external_flows.flow_id` for rational units delta, with the concrete preflow mark link. Canonical integer TEXT avoids SQLite int64/Decimal limits; normalize by GCD, require denominator >0, and verify semantic consistency/content identity on import and startup. A dedicated additive migration is preferable to mutating historical tables/migration checksums. Decimal fields remain bounded reproducible projections, never the threshold/unit authority.
2. Extend the frozen account contract additively with an optional typed exact-NAV evidence record containing those canonical pairs and the mark identity; default absent preserves construction compatibility but a ready Task 3 view requires it. Bind it to the account revision/valuation watermark and exact equity. Task 11's pure assessor then receives exact values through `AccountView`; it need not query SQLite or trust rounded NAV. Do not add float fields or require unrelated model snapshots to serialize these rationals. If projection rounding makes old `high_water_nav >= nav` validation fail despite valid exact ordering, use order-preserving projection rounding for all NAV values and keep exact validation authoritative.
3. Keep the brief's public Decimal helpers for compatibility/reporting and simple exactly representable examples. Persisted flows use the exact rational operation internally: for microdollar equity `e`, capital `f`, and units `a/b`, `U' = a*(e+f)/(b*e)`, reduced by GCD; `delta_U = a*f/(b*e)`. Persist original and new authority in the same operation. With historical `H=h_num/h_den`, compare drawdown by exact integer products `100*e*b*h_den <= 85*1_000_000*a*h_num`; substitute `98` and the day-open pair for daily loss. Integer arithmetic is independent of the Decimal context. Maintain rational H by exact comparison with previous H, and copy exact opening evidence across marks/flows.

These are proposed contract/schema additions, not existing interfaces or approved edits. Conductor should authorize their ownership before Task 3 starts. Set documented input/resource bounds for rational integer text and cross-products, chosen to accommodate the expected account lifetime and frequent flows rather than the existing 38-digit Decimal cap; validate before allocation and fail closed only on genuine resource/arithmetic failure. Bound both numerator and denominator, normalize after each operation, and include ordinary repeated deposits/withdrawals in acceptance. Actual unsupported operations still retain factual immutable evidence and inhibit entries until reconciled; they must never appear silently successful.

Use explicit monetary rounding, conservative valuation/fee directions and a bounded rounding account with retained exact residual evidence. Do not confuse `ROUND_DOWN` (toward zero) with `ROUND_FLOOR` for negative amounts. Validate actual Decimal types, finiteness, sign, supported precision/scale and int64 bounds before mutation; handle NaN/Infinity, division/quantization/overflow failures transactionally. `Fill` validation alone is not complete economic validation.
